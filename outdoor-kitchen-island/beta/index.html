General notes

  1. This visual is a conceptual representation prepared by Heights Contracting for design review and client approval. Dimensions, finishes and colours are indicative and remain subject to confirmation on site.
  2. The pergola is a timber structure with posts, corner braces, side beams and closely spaced rafters. A PVC roofing sheet is laid over the timber to shield it from water, with a fall of about 5% toward the front edge for drainage.
  3. The finishes shown (worktops, flooring, wall panel, cladding, cabinet fronts, timber and PVC colour) are the options selected in the materials panel and are listed in this summary.
  4. Material textures are representative photographs of natural products. Actual samples vary in colour and pattern and should be approved before ordering.
  5. Final drawings, technical details and quantities will be issued after on-site measurement and approval of the layout.
